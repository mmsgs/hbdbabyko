Pour l’âme précieuse Sameintha,

Hello, baby! It has been four years since we met and became friends—best friends. We met when we were only thirteen and fourteen years old, and now you’re turning eighteen. Words are not enough to capture the four beautiful years I’ve shared with you, nor can they properly explain how much your presence has meant to me—how you became the person I could run to whenever it felt like the world was against me. You are truly my sister from another mother. Who I am today is because of you—your glow can light up any room you step into, and I hope you always know that.

In 8th grade, I remember you saying you wanted to be my friend because of the Yeonjun photocard on my bag. I am so thankful I brought that photocard that day, because through it, I found you! Our section was so random, but it felt like home because of you. You never made me feel like an outcast or an outsider. We grew so close—going from being seatmates in class and at the canteen to you becoming the person I’d always ask to go gala with us at Southwoods Mall. I still remember dropping our bags at OJO just to go to GongCha or wherever the day took us.

In 9th grade, sadly, we were separated—they hated to see us coming, I swear! I was so sad to be separated from you because I really didn’t know anyone else there. Yet, whenever break or lunch arrived, you were still the person I ran to.

In 10th grade, omg, we were still separated! But surprisingly, this was the year we became even closer—together with Aya. I was so deeply thankful that our bond remained so strong, because I genuinely don’t know how I would have survived junior high school without you in it. It feels like just yesterday we were talking about senior high school universities and strands and now, as we stand on the edge of graduating Senior High School, we’re already talking about our college programs.

After all those years, here we are in DLSU together—the same school, just in a different place. We are so close, yet I miss catching glimpses of you in the hallways. I miss our spontaneous everyday gala after every exam. I miss our chikahan at GongCha, our monthly dates at Hygge, and our random CR breaks in between classes just because. I miss doing everything with you, but what I miss most of all is your daily presence in my life.

After graduating Junior High School and stepping away from the familiar, I was so scared—terrified, really, that you wouldn’t be there anymore to be my two-calls-away. Before, it was just classrooms and rows of desks separating us; now, it’s cities between us. I always say DLSU treats me better and how much I hated CSA, but I will forever be grateful for my time at CSA because if I hadn't gone there, I never would have found you.

I hope that in every other universe, if they exist, I still get to have you as my best friend. If I don’t, I can’t begin to imagine how empty life would be. I hope you remain the sanctuary I run to in every lifetime—I would trade a thousand lifetimes just to choose you again and again, always.

Happy birthday, Sam! Happy birthday to my very first baby girl in CSA, now turning 18. I wish you all the joy and beauty in the world, even if it means giving up a piece of my own. I hope you know just how deeply you are loved by me, by Aya, and by everyone blessed enough to know you. I am so grateful to have you in my life, my answered prayer over these four years. We may not have grown up together from the start and only met halfway, but sharing these years with you makes it feel like I’ve known you my whole life. You’ve been my home since 2022, and I hope you know you will always have me, too—always. Lastly, never forget you can always run to me. You will always be my baby—and I’ll always be your date and your wife from DLSU Manila. I am forever just two calls away, and you will always have my heart. See you on the DLSU Manila campus next year, Sam! I love you!

De l’âme et du cœur,
— your wife, Mary
